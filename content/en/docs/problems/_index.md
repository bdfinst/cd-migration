Every team migrating to continuous delivery faces obstacles. Most are not unique to your team,
your technology, or your industry. This section catalogs the most common problems teams encounter,
explains why each one blocks your path to CD, and provides a concrete path to fix it.

**Start with the problem you feel most.** Each page links to the practices and migration phases
that address it.
